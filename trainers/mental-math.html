Dealer-specific mental math

Make the math automatic.

This trainer is built around the exact PLO calculation you use in class: triple the last bet, then add the betting trail. Your weak facts and slow answers are saved on this computer and recycled more often.

Train ×3, Triple + Trail, awkward 5-ending numbers, blinds + callers, and realistic POT answers under time pressure.

Mental shortcuts

Cheat Sheets & Mental Shortcuts

Pattern: for a number ending in 5, split off the 5. Example: 85 × 3 = (80 × 3) + (5 × 3) = 240 + 15 = 255.
Bet×3
195 + 85: 195 needs 5 to reach 200. Take 5 from 85, leaving 80. 200 + 80 = 280.
225 + 95: 225 needs 75 to reach 300. Take 75 from 95, leaving 20. 300 + 20 = 320.
Rule: look for the amount needed to reach the next 100. If the second number is large enough, move that amount first, then add the remainder.
POT = Last Bet × 3 + Betting Trail

Example: Last Bet $75, Trail $95.
70 × 3 = 210
5 × 3 = 15
210 + 15 = 225
225 + 95 = 320
POT = $320
×3 ending in 5: remove the 5, triple the round-ten part, then add 15.
Make the next 100: ask “how much gets me to 200 / 300 / 400?” Move that amount first.
Dealer priority: accuracy first, then smooth speed. Repeated correct answers under 5 seconds are the target.
Weak-number rule: anything wrong or slower than your target should return more often until it becomes automatic.
